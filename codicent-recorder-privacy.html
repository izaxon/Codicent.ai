Codicent Recorder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Who we are

Codicent Recorder is provided by Codicent Inside AB, Eklanda Skog 104, 431 49 Mölndal, Sweden. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@codicent.com.

Data the app accesses and sends

The app requests microphone permission only to record audio when you start a recording or enable a recording feature. Recordings are stored on your device until you delete them or choose to delete them after upload.

When you configure a Codicent server and choose to upload, the app sends the recording, its filename, recording time and the tags you select to that server. The server address and API token are entered by you. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not sell audio or other personal data.

Why we use the data

Audio and related metadata are used only to provide the recording, upload and Codicent transcription functions you choose. A Codicent server may create a transcript when you use a transcription tag; the server and its project owner determine any further processing and retention of uploaded audio, messages and transcripts.

Sharing and security

The app shares recording data only with the Codicent server you configure. You should configure an HTTPS server. Android permission controls limit microphone access, and the app keeps local recordings in its app-specific storage. Do not share your API token.

Retention and deletion

You can delete recordings from the app before upload and can enable automatic local deletion after a successful upload. To request deletion of data already uploaded to a Codicent server, contact the owner of that Codicent project or us at the address above. Retention on a configured Codicent server is governed by that server's project and applicable legal obligations.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The current version is published at this URL.
